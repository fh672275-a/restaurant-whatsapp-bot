## 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. Don't Overpromise
- "100% bug-free" mat bolen
- "Hum koshish karte hain best service ki" bolen

### 2. Set Realistic Expectations
- WhatsApp kabhi kabhi disconnect ho sakta hai
- AI 95% time perfect jawab deta hai
- Server kabhi kabhi down ho sakta hai

### 3. Build Relationships
- Har customer ko personally janein
- Un ki problems suniye
- Quick fix ka wada karein

### 4. Scale Slowly
- 50 se zyada customers mat lein abhi
- Pehle 50 ko perfect karein
- Phir 100, 200 kar ke badhein
